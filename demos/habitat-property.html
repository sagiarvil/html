The residence

A house that opens gradually.

Oak House sits behind a restrained brick wall on one of the North Quarter's quieter streets. From the entrance, the plan unfolds toward a long garden rather than revealing everything at once. The original structure was kept, but the interior was opened through a sequence of calm rooms, framed views and warm material changes.

The kitchen and family space run across the back of the house, with full-height glazing toward mature olive and maple trees. A separate sitting room keeps evening use quieter. Upstairs, the main bedroom occupies the garden side with a private terrace, while three further rooms allow family, guests and work to coexist without compromise.

Architecture is quiet here. The garden does most of the talking.
