Definition

A market term for structuring content so answer-oriented systems can more easily identify clear, useful responses.

What does Google say?

Google recognizes AEO as a term used in the market, while stating that success in generative AI Search still relies on Search fundamentals, helpful content and technical eligibility.

What it means for AI systems

Answer extractability improves when headings, paragraphs, definitions, evidence and page intent make the answer clear without losing context.

Why it matters to a website owner

If a customer asks a direct question, the site should make its useful answer easy to identify and verify.

How HTML&HTML measures it

HTML&HTML evaluates answer-first structure, heading-to-answer continuity, self-contained sections, evidence and structured content signals.

What it does not guarantee

There is no special AEO markup required by Google Search and no fixed word-count formula guarantees extraction.
