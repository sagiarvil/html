TRUST SURFACE

Privacy

The public scanner is designed to inspect public HTTP/HTTPS surfaces. It does not require account registration for a scan and it does not intentionally request private network targets.

Policy

The service may receive the domain/URL submitted for scanning and public responses required to compute the result. Access-gated features can use server-side provider credentials; those credentials are not exposed in public page source.

Boundary

Do not submit secrets, intranet targets or credential-bearing URLs.
