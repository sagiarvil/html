FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Website AI Readiness?

Website AI Readiness is the technical state of a site that allows AI models and autonomous agents to crawl, understand, and cite its content accurately.

Does llms.txt guarantee AI visibility?

No. llms.txt is an evolving proposal that guides LLMs to clean markdown documentation. It is evaluated with appropriate weight under PROPOSAL status.

Can I fix readiness issues myself?

Yes. The free audit provides exact issues, affected URLs, and evidence snippets. For automated remediation contracts, use the $99 Fix Mandate.
