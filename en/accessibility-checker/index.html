FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does html lang matter?

Without html lang, screen readers may read English text with a French or Turkish pronunciation engine, making it unintelligible.

Should decorative images have alt text?

Decorative images should have an empty alt='' attribute, not a missing alt attribute.

Why are button divs problematic?

A div with an onclick handler cannot be navigated with Tab and activated with Enter/Space by keyboard users.
