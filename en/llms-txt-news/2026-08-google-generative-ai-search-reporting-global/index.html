AI Search Visibility Can Now Be Measured Separately in Search Console

Page-level visibility in generative AI search moves GEO and AEO measurement closer to observed evidence and away from guesswork.

2026-08-31AI SEARCH MEASUREMENT

What changed?

The dedicated performance view makes it possible to observe impressions from generative AI Search experiences together with the pages, countries, devices and dates associated with that visibility. The practical shift is from asking whether AI can see a site to measuring which URLs actually earn exposure.

Why it matters to a website owner

AI visibility strategy no longer has to depend only on third-party estimates. Website owners can compare technical changes against observed visibility changes.

Technical impact

Changes to sitemaps, canonicals, indexability, internal links, content quality and entity signals can be compared before and after deployment. For HTML&HTML this is field visibility evidence outside the free technical diagnostic itself.

What should be checked now?

  1. Store a recurring baseline from the generative AI report.
  2. Compare visible URLs against your canonical and sitemap inventory.
  3. Map deployment dates to visibility trends instead of attributing changes from memory.
EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

An impression is observed visibility, not a guarantee of recommendation, click or conversion.
