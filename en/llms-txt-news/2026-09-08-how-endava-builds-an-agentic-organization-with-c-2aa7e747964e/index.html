Executive Summary & Core Development

Endava announced a transition to agentic workflows within its software engineering lifecycle through Codex integration. This architectural shift aims to compress traditionally lengthy requirements analysis and planning phases from weeks down to hours.

Deploying AI-powered coding assistants and autonomous agents at an enterprise scale accelerates the entire software development lifecycle (SDLC) while fundamentally altering developer productivity and delivery velocity. This development reinforces the broader industry trend of large-scale technology service providers adopting autonomous agent architectures as a core operational standard.
