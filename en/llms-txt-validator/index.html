What should an llms.txt validator prove?

File presence is not success. A useful validator separates syntax, real reachability and discovery signals rather than collapsing them into one opaque pass/fail result.

FAQ

Hard boundaries

Does llms.txt guarantee Google rankings?

No. The score represents measured technical conditions, not ranking, traffic or AI citation guarantees.

Is llms-full.txt required?

No. llms-full.txt is a widely used industry convention but is not treated as a mandatory element of the llms.txt proposal.

Do you scan private or localhost targets?

No. Private/reserved destinations, credential-bearing URLs and non-standard ports fail closed to reduce SSRF risk.
