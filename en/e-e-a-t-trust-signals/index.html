Definition

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness: a quality-evaluation concept used in Google's guidance and quality rater framework.

What does Google say?

Google says E-E-A-T can help creators self-assess content quality. Search quality raters use it to evaluate results, but rater data is not used directly as a ranking algorithm score.

What it means for AI systems

Clear authorship, first-hand experience, sourcing, organization identity, editorial accountability and factual accuracy make content easier to trust and verify.

Why it matters to a website owner

For commercial and high-stakes topics, visitors and systems both need evidence of who is responsible for the information and why it should be trusted.

How HTML&HTML measures it

HTML&HTML observes authorship, About/Contact/Privacy presence, organization/person schema, citations, freshness and trust signals; it does not claim to measure Google's internal E-E-A-T score.

What it does not guarantee

E-E-A-T is not a single public numeric Google ranking factor that third-party tools can read.
