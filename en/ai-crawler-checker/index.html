FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot?

GPTBot collects data for model training, whereas OAI-SearchBot powers real-time search queries inside ChatGPT Search.

Should I block all AI crawlers?

Blocking all crawlers prevents both model training and citation in AI search engines. A granular policy is generally recommended.

How does HTML&HTML test robots.txt?

It parses the canonical robots.txt according to RFC 9309 and evaluates specific user-agent rules against public URLs.
