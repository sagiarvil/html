Definition

A market term for work intended to improve visibility and source eligibility in generative AI search experiences.

What does Google say?

Google explicitly recognizes the term GEO, but says that from Google Search's perspective optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO and there are no special technical requirements for AI Overviews or AI Mode.

What it means for AI systems

Useful GEO work focuses on eligibility, retrieval, original information, trust, clear entities, usable links and measurable referrals rather than unsupported AI ranking hacks.

Why it matters to a website owner

How HTML&HTML measures it

What it does not guarantee

GEO is not a Google-approved shortcut and it cannot guarantee inclusion in AI-generated answers.
