# HTML&HTML Licensing

Final commercial license terms must match the approved sales channel and seller-approved rights.

Before checkout is activated, the published license must explicitly cover:
- personal use
- commercial use
- client and agency use
- modification rights
- number of permitted end products or deployments
- redistribution restrictions
- raw source-code resale restrictions
- support and update scope

No previous Creative Commons, perpetual-buyout, unlimited-deployment or indemnification language should be treated as an active HTML&HTML sales promise unless it is explicitly re-approved in the final license.
