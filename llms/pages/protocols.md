## 1. The 7 Enterprise Optimization Pillars
- **SEO (Search Engine Optimization)**: Foundational crawlability, clean semantic HTML, single H1 hierarchy, indexable robots.txt directives, and canonical linking.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**: Statistical backing, atomic answers, contrastive analysis, and cross-encoder relevance maximization.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)**: Direct answer extractability for conversational interfaces and voice assistants via FAQPage and HowTo microdata.
- **LLMO (Large Language Model Optimization)**: Multi-tier LLMS.TXT architecture, token-efficient Markdown representations, and pre-training corpus presence.
- **AAO (Autonomous Agent Optimization)**: Frictionless discovery for AI agents via structured JSON surfaces, OpenAPI contracts, and Agent-to-Agent protocols.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation Readiness)**: Chunk-optimized content structure, high semantic density, and explicit context preservation.
- **E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)**: Verified author profiles, institutional sameAs rings (Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase), and formal methodology disclosures.
