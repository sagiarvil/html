01 / STATUS

Open product profile, not a third-party web standard.

This profile is authored and maintained by HTML&HTML. It does not claim endorsement by IETF, W3C, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity or any other external organization. External requirements retain their own source class and canonical source.

FACT

Measured evidence

HTTP, HTML, headers, robots and link responses are direct observations. They can be reproduced against the same public target.

RULE

Normative force stays visible

Standards, first-party vendor guidance, proposals and HTML&HTML heuristics are never presented as if they carry equal authority.

UNKNOWN

No forced certainty

Unavailable evidence is excluded from the relevant denominator. NOT_MEASURED is neither a pass nor a fail.

03 / CONFORMANCE

What a conforming HTML&HTML scan must preserve.

These are implementation invariants, not marketing claims.

01

Evidence provenance

Every finding exposes severity, confidence, source class and evidence. A vendor recommendation cannot be silently upgraded into an industry standard.

02

Unknown-state integrity

UNKNOWN and NOT_MEASURED are excluded from the relevant scoring denominator. Missing data cannot improve or damage a score by assumption.

03

Security fail-closed

Local, private, reserved, credential-bearing and prohibited network targets are rejected. Redirects cannot be used to pivot a public scan into private address space.

04

No fabricated field performance

LCP, INP and CLS are not inferred from static HTML. Without reliable field/lab data they remain NOT_MEASURED.

05

No fake agent endpoints

Optional A2A, MCP or similar discovery surfaces must not be published merely to increase a readiness score. A capability is advertised only when a maintained contract exists.

06

No ranking guarantee

A technical score describes measured conditions. It does not guarantee rankings, traffic, revenue, inclusion, answer selection or citation by any AI/search product.
