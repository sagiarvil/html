# HTML&HTML AI Search Visibility Positioning Contract

## Category promise
HTML&HTML prepares websites to compete for visibility, citation eligibility and recommendation opportunity in AI search experiences by measuring website-side blockers.

Customer-language promise (TR):

> HTML&HTML, web sitenizi yapay zeka arama sonuçlarında görünmeye ve tavsiye edilme fırsatı kazanmaya hazırlar.

This is an opportunity/readiness promise, not an outcome guarantee.

## Priority vocabulary
User-facing Turkish content intentionally foregrounds: yapay zeka, AI, yapay zeka arama sonuçları, tavsiye edilmek, kaynak gösterilmek, ChatGPT'de görünmek, yapay zeka görünürlüğü and yapay zeka kaynaklı trafik.

Technical authority vocabulary: GEO, AEO, LLMO, AAO, RAG, E-E-A-T, llms.txt, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data, entity graph, answer extractability, query fan-out, source eligibility and AI mention tracking.

## Official Google boundary
Google Search recognizes GEO and AEO as market terminology but states that generative AI Search optimization remains SEO from Google's perspective. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode have no special additional technical requirements beyond Search eligibility and best practices. Google Search also states that it does not use llms.txt or other special AI text files for inclusion in Google Search or its generative AI features.

Therefore HTML&HTML must never claim:
- Google approval of HTML&HTML, GEO, AEO, LLMO or AAO.
- access to private Google ranking data.
- that llms.txt improves Google Search ranking or is required for Google AI Search.
- guaranteed AI recommendations, citations, traffic, customers or revenue.
