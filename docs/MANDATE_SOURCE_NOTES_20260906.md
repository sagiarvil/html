The customer mandate is used as a commercial framing input, not as an authority source. Its strongest accepted ideas are:

- lead with user language: yapay zeka / AI / arama sonuçlarında görünmek / tavsiye edilme fırsatı
- use GEO + AEO + LLMO + AAO + RAG + E-E-A-T + llms.txt actively and explain them
- make llms.txt a high-interest acquisition surface while keeping its proposal status explicit
- lead with the customer outcome, then explain the mechanism
- keep one free diagnosis CTA and one clear $149 implementation CTA
- create an authoritative bilingual reference glossary
- use loss-awareness through questions, not fabricated competitor claims

The following mandate elements are intentionally not adopted without independent evidence or because they conflict with current production contracts:

- fabricated testimonials or case studies
- unsupported keyword-volume, market-size, CAGR, zero-click, conversion, or adoption statistics
- claims that llms.txt is a Google ranking requirement or a universal standard
- claims that AEO/GEO/LLMO/AAO/RAG are direct Google ranking factors
- claims that E-E-A-T is a single readable numeric ranking factor
- guaranteed AI recommendations, citations, traffic, customers or revenue
- unapproved $49/$499 subscription tiers or digital-product pricing
- replacing the current stack with Next.js/PostgreSQL/Redis solely because a mandate suggests it
- replacing the canonical 12-engine architecture with a new incompatible scoring model

Primary external claims are instead grounded in the public source registry and official Google/OpenAI guidance.
