# HTML&HTML — AI Search Customer Positioning Contract

## Category
HTML&HTML owns **AI Search Visibility / Yapay Zeka Arama Görünürlüğü** as the commercial category. Technical SEO remains foundational infrastructure, not the primary customer promise.

## Customer expectation
Primary Turkish promise:

> HTML&HTML, web sitenizi yapay zeka arama sonuçlarında görünmeye ve tavsiye edilme fırsatı kazanmaya hazırlar.

Primary customer question:

> Müşteriniz yapay zekaya “Kimi tavsiye edersin?” diye soruyor. Cevapta siz var mısınız?

The English tree uses the equivalent expectation: AI discovery, citation/source eligibility, recommendation opportunity and referral readiness.

## Active vocabulary
Customer-facing pages actively use and explain:

- Yapay zeka / AI
- Yapay zeka arama sonuçları / AI search visibility
- Tavsiye edilme fırsatı / recommendation opportunity
- Kaynak gösterilme / citation and source eligibility
- llms.txt
- GEO — Generative Engine Optimization
- AEO — Answer Engine Optimization
- LLMO — Large Language Model Optimization
- AAO — AI Agent Optimization
- RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- E-E-A-T
- Sitemap, robots.txt, canonical, structured data and internal links as the technical foundation

## Evidence boundary
The positioning is expectation-led, not guarantee-led.

HTML&HTML never claims control over external AI or search systems. It does not guarantee rankings, AI recommendations, citations, mentions, traffic, customers or revenue.

Google's 2026 guidance is represented accurately:

- Google recognizes GEO and AEO as market terms, while treating optimization for Google's generative AI Search as SEO.
- Google describes RAG/grounding and query fan-out in its generative AI Search guidance.
- Google emphasizes crawlability, internal links, textual content, structured-data consistency, unique/non-commodity people-first content and Search Console measurement.
- Google Search does not require or specially use llms.txt for inclusion in its generative AI Search features.

OpenAI guidance is represented accurately:

- Public websites can appear in ChatGPT Search.
- OAI-SearchBot access can help content be discovered, surfaced, cited and linked.
- ChatGPT referral traffic can be measured through the referral UTM OpenAI documents.
- Accessibility semantics can help browser agents understand interactive website elements.

llms.txt remains labeled **PROPOSAL**. It is sold as an emerging machine-readable knowledge surface and validator opportunity, not as a Google ranking requirement.

## Commercial boundary
Free diagnosis exposes evidence and readiness context. The implementation-grade product remains the **$149 Full Site Fix Mandate**. No unapproved $49/$99/$499 tiers or fake social proof are introduced by this positioning layer.

## Architecture invariants
This content layer must not change:

- canonical 12-engine score
- 13 non-scoring Intelligence Audits
- seven readiness lenses
- Deep Crawl maximum 50 public HTML pages
- SSRF and fail-closed security controls
- paid mandate entitlement boundary
- AI Mention Tracker paid/configuration boundary

## Build contract
`scripts/apply_customer_positioning.py` runs after authority generation and the existing static commercial injector. It rewrites customer-facing H1s, leads, titles, descriptions and relevant CTAs on canonical commercial/tool/guide pages while leaving legal surfaces untouched.

CI fails if key customer-facing routes lose the positioning, required AI terminology disappears, or unsupported Google/AI guarantee claims are introduced.
