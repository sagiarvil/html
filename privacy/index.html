Privacy: Scan and Payment Data

How HTML&HTML processes scan, order and Paddle payment-integration data.

1. Data we process

Free and paid scans may process the submitted domain/URL, public HTTP responses, scan findings, scores, evidence records, scan identifiers and technical logs. We do not request passwords, private keys or private-network credentials.

2. Payment data

When live sales begin, payment-card and payment credentials are processed by Paddle. HTML&HTML does not collect full card numbers or card security codes. We may process transaction IDs, payment status and limited buyer contact details supplied by Paddle where needed for fulfillment and support.

3. Purposes

Data is used to run scans, display results, generate the post-payment ZIP, verify order-bound download entitlement, prevent abuse, investigate security events and provide support.

4. Public-web and SSRF security

The platform analyzes public HTTP/HTTPS targets only. localhost, private/reserved IP ranges, metadata endpoints and private DNS resolutions are rejected fail-closed.

5. Retention and sharing

Data is kept only as needed to operate the service, secure it and meet legal obligations. Paddle may process payment data as Merchant of Record and infrastructure providers may process technical data in their respective roles. We do not sell personal data for advertising.

6. Rights and contact

For applicable access, correction or deletion requests, contact contact@htmlandhtml.com. Mandatory legal retention requirements may still apply.
